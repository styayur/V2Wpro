# Changelog

## v0.3.0 — Kinetogenesis

Adds metric movement profiles, independently sampled contact surfaces, departure curvature constraints, a finite architectural district, editable preview rules and browser GLB export. The downstream diagnostic reader verifies replay identity and summarizes measured flight. Reproduction commands and compatibility limits are in [the contact study](docs/contact-study.md).

## v0.2.0 — Morphogenesis

WorldIR 2.0 moves production rules into the document. The same bounded interpreter handles named prototypes, nested arrays, radial placements, transformations, seeded jitter and separate collision proxies. A 3D graph describes the flight routes. The compiler verifies every edge against the player sphere and checks reachability from spawn.

Adds a reusable multi-source review format, partial revisions, seed variants, source digest checks, an asset inspection and exchange workflow, and three spatial studies. Browser evaluation records action responses, real traversal, cell disposal and return-trip memory samples. WorldIR 1.0 keeps its original interpreter and examples.

## v0.1.0

First standalone release. Introduces WorldIR 1.0, a local FFmpeg review workflow, three seeded corridor grammars, a Three.js adapter, a Blender batch converter, a coding-agent skill and Playwright evaluation. Includes an original video-to-world replay and static browser showcase.
