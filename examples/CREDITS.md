The six Topogenesis sweep and loft modules, socket frames, collision proxies, Switchyard recipes and GLB exports are original V2Wpro work under MIT. Measured Skimmer flight traces and the harness distributed in the joint flight-evidence archive belong to the Skimmer-Scraper project under AGPL-3.0-only. That archive includes its source revision, license and file hashes. The two projects exchange data files.

**Third-party reference for Canopy.** Global Donald uploaded *Veo-3 animation example - Chernobyl decaying room* on 10 August 2025 and dedicated the clip to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The uploader identifies Google Veo-3 as the model. The original prompt describes the furniture, peeling paint, an intended half-turn and sound design. The [local provenance record](canopy-provenance.json) retains the full original prompt and the four keyframe timestamps. The copied description text is credited to Global Donald and Wikimedia Commons contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Original prompt, model, upload history and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Veo-3_animation_example_-_Chernobyl_decaying_room.webm&oldid=1069385069) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The project downloaded the [original 1920 × 1080 WebM](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/ff/Veo-3_animation_example_-_Chernobyl_decaying_room.webm) (SHA-256 `1dc48847867eda5a3b9013248842f5ed7bfd0f05a236f694e9667a8291c878f2`). The distributed [canopy-source.mp4](canopy-source.mp4) preserves the eight-second shot at 640 × 360, encoded in H.264 with the audio removed. The review binds to this derivative's SHA-256. The source contact sheet in the case study derives from the same CC0 clip.

The three WorldIR 2.0 reviews, authored briefs and browser captures are original project work under MIT. The Lattice review includes a byte-identical copy of the MIT beacon in its assets directory so that the CLI can compile the review in a separate directory.

The original dash.mp4 motion specification, authored with tools/dash-study.py under MIT, illustrates the acceleration envelope used by Motion Playground. Its timings are known from the authored source.

All original example media, WorldIR documents, procedural rules, review bindings and the logo are by the V2Wpro contributors and distributed under the repository MIT License. reference.mp4 is a four-second original, synthetic 2D perspective animation rendered by tools/storyboard.py using Pillow and FFmpeg, independent of the game renderer. keyframe.png is the one-second frame decoded from that clip. flight-analysis.json records the actual agent-assisted frame review. flight-design.json supplies authored metric constraints. arcade.json is the compiled output. island.json and gate.json are authored examples.

assets/beacon.glb is the octagonal material-bearing pillar generated by tools/blender.py. Captures and recordings in the evaluation derive from these original works. No reference-repository assets were imported. The static build includes THIRD-PARTY.txt for bundled JavaScript dependencies.
