# Sources and licenses

The original dash.mp4 motion specification, authored with tools/dash-study.py under MIT, illustrates the acceleration envelope used by Motion Playground. Its timings are known from the authored source.

All original example media, WorldIR documents, procedural rules, review bindings and the logo are by the V2Wpro contributors and distributed under the repository MIT License. reference.mp4 is a four-second original, synthetic 2D perspective animation rendered by tools/storyboard.py using Pillow and FFmpeg, independent of the game renderer. keyframe.png is the one-second frame decoded from that clip. flight-analysis.json records the actual agent-assisted frame review. flight-design.json supplies authored metric constraints. arcade.json is the compiled output. island.json and gate.json are authored examples.

assets/beacon.glb is the octagonal material-bearing pillar generated by tools/blender.py. Captures and recordings in the evaluation derive from these original works. No reference-repository assets were imported. The static build includes THIRD-PARTY.txt for bundled JavaScript dependencies.
